## What They Do

Larian Studios is a Belgian video game developer and publisher specializing in **deep, systemic role-playing games (
RPGs)**. They develop their own proprietary engine — the **Divinity Engine** — and have built a reputation for
ambitious, player-driven RPGs with emergent gameplay systems.

## Upcoming Project: *Divinity*

- **Announced:** December 2025 via cinematic trailer
- **Tagline:** *"The gods are silent. Rivellon bleeds. New powers stir."*
- **Description:** "An RPG with greater breadth & depth than ever before" — built by the BG3 team
- **Not** *Baldur's Gate 4* — Larian explicitly canceled BG3 DLC and ruled out BG4
- **Not confirmed** as *Divinity: Original Sin 3*, though set in the same universe (Rivellon)
- Swen Vincke described it as "different than you think" but "still familiar"

## Business & Financial Health

- **Privately held** and independent — no public shareholders
- **Tencent** acquired a minority stake (undisclosed amount, ~2023)
- *Baldur's Gate 3* was a massive commercial success (sold millions; exact figures undisclosed)
- *Divinity: Original Sin* nearly bankrupted the company before its success — a cautionary tale that shaped their
  risk-aware culture
- Revenue from back catalog (DOS, DOS2, BG3) provides stable funding for new projects

## Technology

- **Divinity Engine:** Proprietary in-house engine, continuously developed since *Divinity: Original Sin* (2014)
- Key tech areas: ECS architecture, memory management, networking, physics, animation systems, data-oriented design
- Supports PC, Mac, Linux, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch
- Cross-save and cross-play infrastructure (Larian Account system)

## Company Culture & Workplace

- **International:** 7 studios, 500+ employees, "small-studio feel" maintained across locations
- **Collaborative:** Open environment where ideas are encouraged; community feedback actively listened to
- **Passion-driven:** Known for high-quality RPGs; employees share hobbies and passions
- **Active recruitment:** Currently hiring across all studios and disciplines (engine, art, design, audio, cinematics,
  QA, etc.)
- **Social media presence:** 251K+ LinkedIn followers; active on X/Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Twitch,
  Discord, TikTok, Bluesky
